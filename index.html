A world in motion

Since
1950.

Since the first FIA Formula One World Championship in 1950, the sport has evolved from daring post-war Grand Prix racing into a global contest of drivers, constructors and ever-advancing technology.

1950

The first World Championship race is held at Silverstone. Giuseppe Farina becomes the inaugural Drivers' Champion.

1958

The Constructors' Championship is introduced. Vanwall wins the first title; Mike Hawthorn takes the Drivers' crown.

1970s

New safety standards and aerodynamic ideas transform the cars and circuits—and keep reshaping the sport.

2008

Singapore hosts Formula 1's first night race, adding a spectacular new setting to the calendar.

2014

The V6 turbo-hybrid era begins, putting energy recovery and efficiency at the heart of power-unit performance.

2022

Ground-effect aerodynamics return, with new car designs intended to help drivers race more closely.

2026

A new generation of cars and power units ushers in another major technical chapter for Formula 1.

Championship icons

Two legends.
Seven titles each.

Lewis Hamilton and Michael Schumacher share the record for the most Formula 1 Drivers' World Championships. Their careers span different eras, teams and generations of racing.

7 WORLD TITLES · GREAT BRITAIN

Lewis Hamilton

Champion with McLaren in 2008 and with Mercedes from 2014 to 2020, Hamilton shares the record of seven Drivers' titles. His 105 Grand Prix victories are the most in Formula 1 history.

7 WORLD TITLES · GERMANY

Michael Schumacher

After back-to-back titles with Benetton, Schumacher won five consecutive championships with Ferrari from 2000 to 2004. His 91 Grand Prix wins stood as the record until Hamilton surpassed it.

Records & rivalries

The names
in the history books.

From the first race at Silverstone to modern title deciders, F1 history is written by world champions, legendary constructors and the circuits that test them.

1950

The first champion

Giuseppe “Nino” Farina wins the inaugural Drivers' World Championship after the first season of the new series.

1958

Constructors count

Vanwall becomes the first Constructors' World Champion, adding a team title to the drivers' championship.

2008

A last-corner title

Lewis Hamilton clinches his first championship on the final lap of the season finale in Brazil.

2025

A new champion

Lando Norris wins his first Drivers' title, while McLaren claims its 10th Constructors' Championship.

Grand Prix moments

Wins that made history.

A few races that capture the drama, speed and unpredictability of Formula 1.

1950

Silverstone · The first race

Giuseppe Farina wins the first Formula One World Championship Grand Prix, launching a new era of motor racing.

1976

Fuji · A title in the rain

James Hunt secures the championship by a single point after Niki Lauda retires from the rain-soaked finale.

2008

São Paulo · The last lap

Hamilton passes Timo Glock in the final corners to secure his first world title by a single point.

2012

Valencia · A wild finish

Fernando Alonso wins from 11th on the grid in a race of retirements and late drama.

2021

Abu Dhabi · The title decider

Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton arrive level on points for the finale; Verstappen takes his first championship.

2025

Abu Dhabi · A first crown

Lando Norris finishes third in the finale to clinch the Drivers' Championship by two points.
